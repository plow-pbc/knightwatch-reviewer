About these metrics
Bars show each stage's median start and finish; their overlap shows parallel work. Duration labels are median stage durations. The finish marker includes setup and posting.
Runs, skips, gated and gap cover 7 days. Specialist finding and edited-later rates are in the Specialists card below.
Gated is how often a stage was the last to finish before the final review started; red stages gated at least 25% of the runs they ran in. Gap is the median time between a stage finishing and the final review starting; it is not free delay, because later stages may depend on it.
Running reviews are flagged after 3× the median. Queue waits over 1h are long; a queue is stalled only when no review has started in 30m and an active reviewer is free.

About these metrics
Edited later = specialist runs whose published finding was followed by an edit to a cited file, over all runs. Later edits do not prove a finding was fixed. Weeks with fewer than 30 runs are not drawn. ◆ marks a prompt update; hover for the commit. Δ compares the 4 weeks after the last prompt update with the 4 weeks before. Prompt updates are dated by commit, which can precede deployment.
Value vs. cost plots 30-day edited-later rate against 7-day median runtime; bubble size is runs, and the dashed lines are the median specialist. In the funnel, a short blue bar is a timid prompt and a long blue bar over a short green one is noise.
