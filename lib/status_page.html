About these metrics
Bars show each stage's median start and finish; their overlap shows parallel work. Duration labels are median stage durations. The finish marker includes setup and posting.
Runs and skips cover 7 days. Finding and later-edit rates cover 30 days: reviews with a published finding / specialist runs, then reviews with a later edit to a cited file / reviews with a finding. Later edits do not prove a finding was fixed.
Running reviews are flagged after 3× the median. Queue waits over 1h are long; a queue is stalled only when no review has started in 30m and an active reviewer is free.
